# TBM — AI as a Managed Investment

> **Capstone 3** of the [AI × FinOps Journey](https://github.com/gustavotche/ai-finops-journey).
> A TBM (Technology Business Management) model that translates AI token spend into business value for executive decision-making.

## Problem

AI spend is often an opaque cost to leadership. This project delivers a model and a decision framework that connect token spend to AI services and business units, helping answer "when does an agent pay for itself?".

## Deliverables

- TBM model: cost pools → towers → IT services → business units (Taxonomy 5.0)
- Token spend → AI services → ROI per business unit
- Decision framework: cost-per-outcome / payback
- Article on AI cost management for decision-makers
- Reusable template
